What is your city hiding from you?

There are no wrong answers. Answer how you usually are, not how you'd like to be. It takes about four minutes.

Four possible identities:
The Culture Detective · The Story Hunter · The Local's Table · The Beautifully Lost

The science behind it

Each of the six traits is measured by three statements, rated on a 5-point agreement scale. The traits draw on established personality and tourism research:

  • Aesthetic sensitivity: the aesthetics facet of Openness to Experience (Five-Factor Model; Costa & McCrae)
  • Intellectual curiosity: epistemic curiosity (Litman) and need for cognition (Cacioppo & Petty)
  • Culinary & social openness: food neophilia (Pliner & Hobden) and interpersonal warmth
  • Novelty seeking: sensation seeking (Zuckerman; Hoyle et al.) and Plog's venturer traveler
  • Need for structure: need for cognitive closure (Webster & Kruglanski)
  • Social energy: Extraversion (Five-Factor Model)

Some statements are reverse-worded so that a habit of agreeing with everything doesn't skew your results.

This is a reflective tool for planning travel, not a clinical or validated psychometric assessment. The statements are our own, adapted from these research concepts.
